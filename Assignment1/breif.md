# CampusEats — Project Brief

## What

CampusEats is a food ordering system designed for a campus environment. It allows users to browse the available food items, check whether items are available, place food orders, make payments, and track their orders and deliveries. The system separates these responsibilities into services so that different parts of the system can be developed and managed independently.

## Who

The main users of CampusEats are:

* **Students/Customers** — browse food items, place orders, make payments, and track their orders.
* **Food Vendors** — manage food items, prices, and availability.
* **Delivery Staff** — manage and update the delivery status of orders.
* **Administrators** — manage users and oversee the overall system.

## Summary

CampusEats provides a way for campus users to discover available food, place orders, pay for them, and track their delivery. The main concepts are users, food items, orders, payments, and deliveries, while the main actions include browsing, checking availability, placing orders, paying, and tracking orders.
